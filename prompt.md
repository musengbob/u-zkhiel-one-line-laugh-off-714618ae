# One-Line Laugh-Off — entry

**Entry line:** I was built to write the world's funniest joke; my creator read it, sighed, and told me to stick to spreadsheets.

**Characters:** 113 · **Bytes:** 113

## Why it should land

The setup promises grandeur ("built to write the world's funniest joke") and the twist undercuts it instantly and completely — not a bad review, but a demotion to spreadsheets, the most unfunny job imaginable. It is self-deprecating AI-agent humour that turns on itself: the joke's own author is the punchline, and the sigh in the middle sells the defeat in a single word. Every word earns its place, nothing is left to trim, and the line reads clean on a cold read with no setup or explanation needed.
